# ClosedLoop AI

- [ClosedLoop AI](https://closedloop.sh/docs/index.md): Connect customer conversations and product behavior, turn them into traceable product insights, and prioritize what matters.
- [Quickstart](https://closedloop.sh/docs/quickstart.md): Connect Gong, inspect your first source-backed product insight, create a Watch, and review future matches in the ClosedLoop AI Inbox.
- [Find and validate product evidence](https://closedloop.sh/docs/guides/find-product-evidence.md): Narrow product insights and context, then trace each finding back to the customer and original source before acting.
- [Monitor a topic with a Watch](https://closedloop.sh/docs/guides/watches-and-inbox.md): Turn a filtered Insights or Context view into recurring personal Inbox matches, then review and act on new evidence.
- [Prioritize product work with Roadmap](https://closedloop.sh/docs/guides/opportunities.md): Use recurring Themes, source evidence, customer impact, and Roadmap coverage to decide what deserves attention.
- [Investigate product behavior](https://closedloop.sh/docs/guides/behavior.md): Review meaningful changes in product use and, when available, compare them with related customer feedback.
- [Understand a customer](https://closedloop.sh/docs/guides/customers.md): Review one customer's product evidence, account summary, people, deals, retention risks, and source-backed product insights in a single profile.
- [Configure your workspace](https://closedloop.sh/docs/guides/company-settings.md): Set domains, products, competitors, customer segments, and AI context so ClosedLoop AI interprets evidence in the right business context.
- [The product decision loop](https://closedloop.sh/docs/concepts/pipeline.md): How ClosedLoop AI helps teams move from customer evidence to product decisions, delivery, and learning.
- [Evidence: Insights, Context, and Behavior](https://closedloop.sh/docs/concepts/insights.md): The three evidence lenses in ClosedLoop AI, what each answers, and why product teams use them together.
- [From evidence to Roadmap](https://closedloop.sh/docs/concepts/opportunities.md): How ClosedLoop AI separates recurring customer demand from planned work so teams can prioritize problems before solutions.
- [Business impact and customer context](https://closedloop.sh/docs/concepts/customer-intelligence.md): How ClosedLoop AI connects product evidence to customers, buying decisions, adoption, retention, and revenue context.
- [Glossary](https://closedloop.sh/docs/glossary.md): Learn the ClosedLoop AI terms used for product insights, evidence, customers, Themes, Features, Roadmap, Watches, Context, Behavior, and MCP.

- [Slack](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/slack.md): Mine Slack for product feedback: customer-shared channels, internal sales chatter, support escalations. Threads become scored insights, no copy-paste.
- [Ada](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/ada.md): See what users asked your AI agent that it couldn't answer, what they escalated, and which questions repeat. Ada conversations as product insights.
- [Intercom](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/intercom.md): Turn Intercom conversations into product insights with ClosedLoop AI: pain points, feature requests, and churn risks extracted and scored automatically.
- [Typeform](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/typeform.md): Pipe Typeform responses into ClosedLoop AI: NPS scores, churn surveys, beta feedback. Open-ended answers become themed insights you can query.

- [Planhat](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/planhat.md): Connect your Planhat workspace to ClosedLoop AI with an API token, verified against your Planhat cluster at connect time so mistakes surface immediately.
- [HubSpot](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/hubspot.md): Sync HubSpot accounts, deals, and lifecycle stages so every insight knows whose deal is at stake, what tier they're on, and which segment they belong to.
- [Salesforce](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/salesforce.md): Sync Salesforce accounts, contacts, opportunities and support records so every product insight carries the customer behind it and the revenue at stake.
- [Microsoft Dynamics 365](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/dynamics-365.md): Sync Dynamics 365 CRM accounts, contacts, opportunities, cases, and owners from Dataverse so product insights carry revenue and customer context.
- [Linear](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/linear.md): Sync your Linear roadmap into ClosedLoop AI: initiatives, projects, and issues connect to customer demand so you see what customers ask for by feature.
- [Productboard](https://closedloop.sh/docs/integrations/productboard.md): Sync your Productboard roadmap into ClosedLoop AI: products, features, and releases connect to customer demand and the insights behind each request.

- [Prompts](https://closedloop.sh/docs/mcp-server/prompts.md): MCP prompts are slash commands the server ships to your agent. Right now there is one: prd, which writes a PRD from real customer evidence.
- [Skills Overview](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/overview.md): Pre-built skills for Claude Code, grouped by the team they serve: product, engineering, sales, marketing, leadership, and customer success.
- [Deep Dive](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/deep-dive.md): Reads every quote, affected customer and CRM record on a single topic, then reports what the underlying problem behind them actually is.
- [PM Prep](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/pm-prep.md): A brief before a customer call: what they have asked for, what is currently broken for them, and the questions worth asking on the call.
- [Scope Guard](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/scope-guard.md): Checks a spec against what customers actually asked for, and reports what is missing, what was invented, and what is already solved.
- [Synthetic Customer](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/synthetic-customer.md): Role-plays one of your customers, grounded only in what they have actually said, so you can test an idea before booking a real call.
- [Competitor Gap](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/competitor-gap.md): Which competitors your customers mentioned, what they said, and whether mentions are rising or falling. From calls and tickets, not desk research.
- [Product Ambassadors](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/product-ambassadors.md): Finds the customers worth inviting to shape a feature: detailed feedback, genuinely engaged, and not the same three names again.
- [Product Ambassador Outreach](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/product-ambassador-outreach.md): Drafts the invitation to a customer you want in a research session, beta or design review, referencing the feedback that qualified them.
- [Technical Spec](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/spec.md): Writes a technical spec in Google's design-doc format: context, goals, the design, and the alternatives you considered and rejected.
- [Bug Blast Radius](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/bug-blast-radius.md): How many customers reported a bug, which ones they are, the revenue behind them, and whether the reports are still coming in today.
- [Sales Playbook](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/sales-playbook.md): How to run the conversation on one topic: the pain in their words, what the workaround costs them, and the objections to expect.
- [Sales Proof](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/sales-proof.md): Ranked packets of evidence for a prospect's concern: which customers raised the same thing, what they said, and what changed for them.
- [Reference Customers](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/reference-customers.md): Finds customers who could take a reference call for a specific prospect, and ranks them so the same three names are not asked every single time.
- [Customer Proof](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/customer-proof.md): Finds customers who said something quotable about your product, with their exact words and the call or support ticket they said it in.
- [Customer Proof Outreach](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/customer-proof-outreach.md): Drafts the permission request to a customer whose words you want to use publicly, referencing what they said and where they said it.
- [Case Study Scout](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/case-study-scout.md): Finds customers who could carry a case study: the ones who described a real, measurable outcome in their own words, on the record.
- [Proof Permissions](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/proof-permissions.md): Records who agreed to be quoted publicly, in what form and when, so the answer to "are we allowed to use this?" is written down, not remembered.
- [Weekly Brief](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/weekly-brief.md): Everything that happened in your customer feedback last week, read in full and summarised into something you can scan in a minute.
- [CSM Prep](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/csm-prep.md): A one-screen brief before a customer call: their open risks, what changed since you last spoke, and any promise still outstanding on your side.
- [Ship Notify](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/ship-notify.md): Finds every customer who asked for the thing you just shipped, and drafts the note telling them, referencing what each one originally said.
- [Watch](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/watch.md): Sets up an alert that fires when something in your customer feedback crosses a threshold you name, delivered to Slack, Teams or email.
- [Send Feedback](https://closedloop.sh/docs/skills/send-feedback.md): Reports a wrong or confusing ClosedLoop AI answer to the team, from wherever you noticed it, with the surrounding context attached to it.
- [API reference](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/introduction.md): Programmatic REST access to your ClosedLoop AI product insights, themes, customers, and analytics. Region-scoped /v1 endpoints with API key auth.
- [Conventions](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/conventions.md): How the ClosedLoop AI REST API behaves across every endpoint: authentication, pagination, error shapes, rate limits, and timestamp formats.
- [List / search insights](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/insights/list-search-insights.md): Search processed product insights by customer, severity, product scope, date, or text, with offset or fixed-watermark incremental pagination.
- [Get an insight](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/insights/get-an-insight.md): Full insight detail with evidence: pain point, workaround, competitor gap, willingness to pay, feature area, product associations, and the verbatim quote.
- [List products](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/products/list-products.md): Current product vocabulary for the authenticated team. Draft and active products are returned by default. Use these immutable IDs with `product_id` filters.
- [List product and feature areas](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/products/list-product-and-feature-areas.md): List active product and feature areas with all-time insight totals and earliest feedback dates, or include archived areas when needed.
- [Check evidence coverage](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/analytics/check-evidence-coverage.md): Check whether a product or area scope has enough evidence for reliable analysis, including coverage dates and windowed insight totals.
- [Time-series trends](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/analytics/time-series-trends.md): Zero-filled time series for a metric over a window. Carries a `feedback_since` coverage boundary so early empty buckets aren't misread as "zero activity".
- [Facet counts](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/analytics/facet-counts.md): Available filter values and their counts over the full dataset (categories, severities, sources). Useful for building filter UIs.
- [List / search themes](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/themes/list-search-themes.md): List or search top-level product themes by status, product scope, evidence date, or meaning, with deterministic sorting and pagination.
- [Get a theme](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/themes/get-a-theme.md): Get one product theme with its severity breakdown, affected customers, supporting insights, buildable features, and merge destination.
- [List / search features](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/features/list-search-features.md): List or search buildable features by theme, product, area, status, or text, with scoped evidence counts and deterministic sorting.
- [Get a feature](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/features/get-a-feature.md): Feature detail with its evidence: severity breakdown, affected customers, and top supporting insights.
- [List / search customers](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/customers/list-search-customers.md): Customers who gave feedback, with CRM context (plan, ARR, active/churned) and **account-family** hierarchy: a parent account and its child properties.
- [Get a customer](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/customers/get-a-customer.md): Get one customer profile with its account summary, product insights, churn state, deals, people, and chronological activity timeline.
- [List customer context](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/context/list-customer-context.md): List strategic customer context such as churn reasons, competitor mentions, and satisfaction, filtered by customer, type, or date.
- [List / search competitors](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/competitors/list-search-competitors.md): List competitors mentioned by customers, including pressure trends and direction, with name search and links to the underlying evidence.
- [Search competitor mentions](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/competitors/search-competitor-mentions.md): Search verbatim competitor mentions by competitor, customer, text, or date, with source details and newest evidence returned first.
- [List integrations](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/integrations/list-integrations.md): List the data-source integrations connected to the current ClosedLoop AI team, including each integration's configuration and sync status.
- [Get an integration](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/integrations/get-an-integration.md): Get one connected data-source integration for the current ClosedLoop AI team, including its configuration, state, and synchronization status.
- [List usage records](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/usage/list-usage-records.md): List ClosedLoop AI credit-consumption records for the current team, filtered by date and returned with pagination for billing transparency.
- [Health check](https://closedloop.sh/docs/api-reference/meta/health-check.md): Check whether the ClosedLoop AI API is reachable and ready to accept requests; this public liveness endpoint requires no authentication.

- [Set up machine-to-machine MCP access](https://closedloop.sh/docs/account/mcp-service-clients.md): Set up secure machine-to-machine MCP access from an internal AI gateway or backend workload to ClosedLoop AI using OAuth client credentials.
- [Billing and credits](https://closedloop.sh/docs/account/billing.md): Reference for how ClosedLoop AI measures credits and where to find current plan, usage, payment, and invoice details.
